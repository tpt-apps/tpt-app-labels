## Description

**Stop paying a monthly subscription just to print labels.**

TPT Labels is a batch QR / DataMatrix / Code128 / Code39 / EAN-13 / UPC-A generator that runs entirely in your browser. Paste in a list of product codes, URLs, or SKUs — one per line, or straight from a CSV — and get a print-ready sheet of labels in seconds.

- **Batch generate** — hundreds of codes at once from pasted text or CSV
- **6 symbologies** — QR Code, Data Matrix, Code 128, Code 39, EAN-13, UPC-A
- **Print-ready sheets** — configurable columns, cell size, print straight to PDF from your browser
- **Export ZIP** — individual SVG or PNG files per code, for use in your own packaging/print workflow
- **100% offline** — once installed, it works with no internet connection ever again
- **No account, no upload, no ongoing subscription** — everything happens on your device; nothing you type is ever sent anywhere
- **Real installer, not a login** — a proper Windows app (Mac/Linux coming), plus a self-host option for phone/tablet

### Who it's for
Small shops, makers, Etsy/market sellers, warehouses, and anyone who needs to print a batch of scannable codes without paying for enterprise label software or a recurring SaaS subscription.

### How it's delivered
Your purchase is a ZIP download containing:
- **The Windows installer** (`.msi`/`.exe`) — double-click to install, run it like any desktop app. No internet connection needed after install.
- **A self-host bundle** for phone/tablet use — drag one folder onto a free instant-hosting page (steps included in the download) to get it on your iPhone/iPad/Android device, installable to your homescreen.

Nothing about this product depends on any ongoing service from us — once installed (or self-hosted), it's yours to keep working indefinitely.

## FAQ

**Does this need an internet connection?**
No, after installing. The desktop app works fully offline; the phone/tablet option needs internet only for the one-time setup step.

**Can I use the generated codes commercially, e.g. on products I sell?**
Yes — generated codes are yours to use however you like.

**What formats can I export?**
Print-ready sheets (print-to-PDF), or a ZIP of individual SVG or PNG files.

**Does it work on my phone or tablet?**
Yes, via the included self-host bundle — full setup steps (about a minute, free, no account) are in the download. It also works on Chromebooks and unusual hardware like RISC-V Linux tablets, since it's standard web technology under the hood.

**Is there a limit on how many codes I can generate?**
No batch-size limit built in; very large batches (1000+) may be slower depending on your device.
