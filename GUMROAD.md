## Description

**Stop paying a monthly subscription just to print labels.**

TPT Labels is a batch QR / DataMatrix / Code128 / Code39 / EAN-13 / UPC-A generator that runs entirely in your browser. Paste in a list of product codes, URLs, or SKUs — one per line, or straight from a CSV — and get a print-ready sheet of labels in seconds.

- **Batch generate** — hundreds of codes at once from pasted text or CSV
- **6 symbologies** — QR Code, Data Matrix, Code 128, Code 39, EAN-13, UPC-A
- **Print-ready sheets** — configurable columns, cell size, print straight to PDF from your browser
- **Export ZIP** — individual SVG or PNG files per code, for use in your own packaging/print workflow
- **100% offline** — install it once, and it works with no internet connection ever again
- **No account, no upload** — everything happens on your device; nothing you type is ever sent anywhere
- **Works on phone, tablet, or desktop** — installable as an app (PWA) on any platform with a browser

### Who it's for
Small shops, makers, Etsy/market sellers, warehouses, and anyone who needs to print a batch of scannable codes without paying for enterprise label software or a recurring SaaS subscription.

### How it's delivered
This is a web app. Your purchase gives you access at [your hosted URL] — install it to your homescreen/desktop for one-click offline access. A downloadable copy is also included for anyone who wants to self-host it.

## FAQ

**Does this need an internet connection?**
Only to load it the first time. After that, it works fully offline.

**Can I use the generated codes commercially, e.g. on products I sell?**
Yes — generated codes are yours to use however you like.

**What formats can I export?**
Print-ready sheets (browser print-to-PDF), or a ZIP of individual SVG or PNG files.

**Does it work on my phone?**
Yes — it's a PWA, so it installs to your homescreen on iOS/Android like a native app.

**Is there a limit on how many codes I can generate?**
No batch-size limit built in; very large batches (1000+) may be slower depending on your device.
