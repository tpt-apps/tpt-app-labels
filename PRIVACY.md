# Privacy — TPT Labels

TPT Labels runs entirely client-side in your browser (WebAssembly).

- **No account, no login.**
- **No data upload.** Every code you generate is rendered locally, in your browser; nothing you type is sent to any server.
- **No telemetry or analytics.**
- **Works offline.** Once the app has loaded once, it keeps working with no internet connection at all (it's an installable Progressive Web App).
- **Cookies:** none set by the app itself. If you install it as a PWA, your browser stores the app shell locally on your device for offline use — that data never leaves your device.
